TRAM CAN QR - PILOT GEMINI MOT CAMERA

1. Giai nen ZIP vao mot thu muc co dinh, vi du C:\TramCanQR-Pilot.
2. Chay CAI-DAT-LAN-DAU.cmd mot lan. May can Python 3.10+ va Internet.
3. Notepad mo config.env. Thay replace-with-key-for-this-station bang API key pilot moi, sau do luu.
4. Chay CHAY-TRAM-CAN.cmd. Edge/Chrome se mo http://127.0.0.1:8080.
5. Cho phep dung camera, chon dung camera, giu can va camera co dinh, bam Space de doc.
6. Khong dong cua so lenh mau den trong luc test.

GUI CHO KY THUAT KHI CO LOI

- Anh loi: %LOCALAPPDATA%\TramCanQR\dataset\factory_raw
- Du lieu/log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\TramCanQR
- Log khoi dong: %LOCALAPPDATA%\TramCanQR\logs\app.log
- Khong gui API key qua Zalo, email hay file log.

CAP NHAT CODE TRONG DOT PILOT

- Dong cua so CHAY-TRAM-CAN.cmd.
- Sao luu thu muc %LOCALAPPDATA%\TramCanQR truoc khi cap nhat.
- Sua/nhan thu muc src moi, sau do chay lai CHAY-TRAM-CAN.cmd. Do source duoc
  cai editable, code moi co hieu luc ngay; khong can cai lai moi lan sua.
- Khong xoa .venv-pilot va khong sua config.env neu ban cap nhat khong yeu cau.

TAO FILE EXE/BO CAI SAU KHI SUA

- File EXE da cai khong tu cap nhat khi sua source.
- Chay BUILD-BAN-MOI.cmd tai thu muc goc source.
- Neu test dat, lay bo cai moi trong dist\installer.
- Dong TramCanQR.exe tren may khach, chay bo cai moi de cai de ban cu.
- config.env, SQLite va cau hinh Supabase tren may khach duoc giu nguyen.

NGHIEM THU TOI THIEU

- Chup 100 luot doc lap tai dung vi tri lap dat.
- Ghi lai gia tri tren can, gia tri phan mem, thoi gian tra ket qua va cac lan khong nhan.
- Khong sua tay ket qua de bien mot lan nhan sai thanh dung.
- Neu sai hoac khong nhan, giu lai anh factory_raw de ky thuat phan tich.
